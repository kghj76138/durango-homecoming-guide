    #2026.09.28.1 public changes UnverifiedLast changed 2026.09.28.1

    Based on the public client available through the launcher update. This does not mean co-op server integration tests or the live server swap are complete.

    • Arena: idle animals keep gathering toward the center and fight the nearest opponent. Fighting, stagger and death animations are no longer overridden by the gathering walk. Spectators' and their pets' attacks and taunts no longer damage match animals or change their target. Bookmaker interaction range and position and automatic camp placement were also adjusted.
    • Pets: added the one-time Ragdoll companion quest, refreshed the animal list for Reins, applied Booster Jelly EXP, and improved remote animal display on older co-op hosts.
    • Dedicated events: added Season 1's separate route and save area, the school's three-item delivery quest, and the April Fools' two paper animal quests with repeat supply.
    • University: fixed per-subject affinity for the Career Guide, a one-time top-up of missing progress in old saves, and the reward window and login step display.
    • Delegated crafting: restored the in-progress appearance and completion effect. Ordinary Ragdolls and Therizinosaurus are excluded from wild spawning and restoration.

    Ragdoll† · Event island · University trust · All quests

    Code, save and network checks and public package verification are done. In-game screen verification of the new events and pets is pending, and this does not mean Lunar New Year or all remaining events are implemented.

    #Co-op islandLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    On a co-op island, a dedicated host program runs the island and several people connect to play together. The principle is the developer instruction “if it's missing on the co-op island, make it the same as single-player” Changed from original (developer decision) — rules, formulas and tables come from One shared set of files for single-player and host compiled into both. So the numbers in this wiki are the same on co-op islands.

    • Weather, volcanic storms and climate fatigue, prefab building, bag capacity, domain land checks, organization Trust tiers, Pioneer missions, closed craters, Warp Accelerator animals and clan members used to be missing or different only on co-op islands; they are now the same.
    • Multiplayer only: since several people share the same island, Connected guests also count toward the boss raid multiplier (Islands & sailing page, “Boss raid multiplier”).
    • Removing the completion balloon of co-op tasks (boss sighting alerts) was handled by the original radio tower server, so map balloons may remain.

    #NPC clan members(Companions)Last changed 2026.09.24.5

    The original has no concept of companions. NPC clan members are a new feature made at the developer's request, and nearly all values are design values or estimates Changed from original (developer decision). Only weapon performance and the Attack grade curve use original data.

    Max members
    15 — the actual cap is the smaller of this and the original clan capacity (clan level)
    Level
    Character level × 1
    Recovery
    Fully depleted Life refills in 5 min (same as pets)
    Downed
    Gets up on its own after 1 min and returns at 50% Life. No permanent death
    Rescue
    Direct rescue by a person 15s · after being down 15s, another clan member goes to rescue · clan member CPR 1.5s
    Following
    Semicircle formation 2 tiles behind the owner (row spacing 1.5 tiles, up to 8 rows). Returns to the owner's side when farther than 20 tiles
    Combat
    Joins fights within 10 tiles, minimum attack interval 1.2s. Guard radius 5 tiles
    Default weapon
    Improvised Wooden Bow, Wooden Crossbow, Improvised Bone Knife, Improvised Two-Handed Bone Hammer
    Name
    Kang Do-hyeon, Park Jeong-u, Oh Ji-hun, Im Hyeon-seok, Yun Se-ra, Seo Da-in, Cha Yu-na, Han Mir

    Clan member gathering†

    Pressing the “Clan member gathering” menu makes clan members gather the natural resources, dead animals and mature crops visible on screen. If domain storage runs short, extra storage boxes are placed free in empty spots; pressing it again stops. Each gather takes 1s, work range 1.25 tiles.
