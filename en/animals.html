    #OverviewLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    The restoration registers 220 species of wild animals (the original animal.txt's 214 species + 6 species the fork cloned or added). 73 species are carnivores, 141 herbivores, and the rest omnivores or scavengers. 64 species can be tamed (drop reins), and 97 species are actually encountered on reachable islands.

    Per-species stats, habitats, butchering yields and latent attributes are in the Animal encyclopedia; sortable, filterable tables are in Animal & island data. This page explains the rules common to all species.

    • Stats are calculated by plugging Combat level and Unstable index multiplier into the formulas in the original animal.txt Original (confirmed).
    • The Unstable index → multiplier mapping, hit/crit/variance, the Attack grade curve, butchering reduction from part breaks, and the Kill EXP formula were set by the restoration because the original server formulas do not survive Estimated Estimated.
    • Butchering yield composition and amounts are estimated because they were server tables absent from the original client; item names and body-part links come from the original data.

    #Stat formula and levelLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    All stats are computed from the original formulas by the in-game formula evaluator (FormulaEvaluator). The formulas have two variables.

    combat_level
    That individual's combat level
    unstable_factor
    Island Unstable index converted to a multiplier = 1 + 0.1 × (index − 1), index 1–10 Estimated

    Life, Attack, Defense and Attack grade mostly have × unstable_factor at the end of the formula and scale with the Unstable index; Evasion (dodge) does not. Regeneration (Life recovered per second) is the life_velocity formula: mostly 0.3% of max Life per second, 1%/s for the Apatosaurus and Tyrannosaurus lines.

    How the level is decided

    • Normal packs: Island level − 2 + (−1~+1), clamped to the species' combat_level_ranges range and to 1–120 Original (confirmed).
    • Crater guardians: Island level + 1~3 (1–120) — 3–5 levels higher than normal packs.
    • Brachiosaurus herds are joined by one juvenile (family grouping) Estimated.

    Default formula for species without data

    Species with empty formulas (missing from the original data) use medium herbivore defaults: Life 1.06 × (lv+24)² × uf, Attack (21.5 + 0.9 lv) × uf, Attack grade 6 lv × uf, Accuracy/Defense 5 lv × uf, Evasion 5 lv, Stun gauge 1.145 × (lv+24)² × uf, with stun clamped to 2–20s and knockdown to 3–15s.

    Example: Raptor (Unstable index 1)

    Formula: Life (0.422 * ((combat_level + 24) ** 2)) * unstable_factor, Attack (15.39 + combat_level * 0.64) * unstable_factor

    #Combat checksLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    When an animal hits a person

    • The player's Evasion ÷ 1,000 (max 25%) is subtracted from the base hit chance 90%. Landing in the subtracted portion gives “Evasion”; outside the base hit chance gives “Missed”.
    • On hit 20% chance of a critical hit(Damage ×1.9 — Original base_critical_damage_bonus 0.9).
    • Damage = Attack × variance (0.85–1.15) → action multiplier → Attack grade curve (animal Attack grade − player Defense) × direction multiplier, minimum 1.
    • Being hit while the player is mid-swing counts as “Countered”.

    Estimated The original server's hit and crit formulas do not survive, so these are restoration rules.

    Attack grade curve (Human·Shared with animals)

    The difference Attack grade − Defense becomes the damage multiplier. From 0 to 100 it connects community-measured points; above 100 it rises by 0.002 per 1 pt up to 180, then by 0.001 per point beyond that, capped at ×2.2. A negative difference gives the reciprocal of the multiplier for the same positive value Estimated.

    #Body parts and part breakLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    Most animals have torso, head and leg parts (large species also a tail). Each part has part HP equal to a fixed share of max Life (hp_ratio, typically torso 0.5 · head 0.2 · legs 0.3 · tail 0.1), reduced by hit damage × the action's part-break coefficient (+ mutilate_plus from learned techniques and gear) Estimated.

    Part defense (defense_ratio) is averaged, weighted by the attack's blunt/pierce/slash ratios, and multiplied into Defense. When a part breaks, “○○'s leg part has been destroyed!” appears and the status effects below are applied Original (confirmed).

    Breaking parts reduces butchering yield

    Following original player accounts (“if a specific part isn't destroyed”), bones from a broken part are reduced. The amounts are Estimated — head → 0 Skulls, legs → half the Leg Bones, torso → half the Ribs. The tail is not reduced.

    BridgeLeg Bone1 → 0 · 2 → 1 · 4 → 2

    The evasion penalty of a broken leg (dodge_plus −40) is subtracted after the evasion cap (0.25), so breaking legs raises hit chance even at high levels (Evasion 290+).

    #Kill EXPLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    Defeating an animal grants character EXP all at once (separate from the combat EXP gained per hit).

    Kill EXP = floor( difficulty × 10 × 1.2^((Level − 1) / 2) )

    difficulty is the species grade from the original animal.txt (Raptor 1 · Dire Wolf 3 · Allosaurus 15 · Tyrannosaurus 39). The original data's exp_amount is all 0 (computed server-side), so the formula was derived from 110 lines of per-species, per-level measurements collected by testers; 107 lines match exactly Estimated. The Unstable index does not apply to EXP.

    #Butchering and yieldsLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    Downed animals are butchered (with a knife) to remove parts. Each butchering yields one of the remaining parts, and the carcass disappears once all are removed. Carcasses left unbutchered disappear after 300s Estimated.

    Parts unlocked by butchering techniques

    Herd hunting decision

    • Hunger: a full stomach empties in 598.8s (original hungry velocity −0.167). After eating, the animal rests for the same time.
    • Individual strength = difficulty × level. A pack attacks only if the combined strength of joining hunters (same species within 4,000) is at least ×0.8 the prey's strength.
    • Hunters do not attack prey more than Stage 3 larger in size grade.
    • Prey search range 3,000, territory radius 6,000; gives up after chasing more than 2,000 outside the territory, and abandons a hunt after 90s.
    • Bitten prey fights back against the hunter (only the two Elephantulus species just flee). If a hunting predator goes down, the pack abandons that hunt.
    • Examples: three Direwolves (3) → Triceratops (10) · three Sabertooths (3) → Mammoth (10) · eight Raptors (1) → Mammoth: they attack. Four Raptors → Mammoth, anything → Tyranno (39): they don't.

    The 0.8 threshold is the lower end of the band (0.8–1.2) where wins and losses split in actual combat simulations (level 30, 8 rounds) — fights are long, around 100s, and the carnivore usually wins Changed from original (developer decision).

    Scavenging and migration

    • Carnivores and scavengers walk to a carcass within detection range (up to 20s), eat for 8s, then rest 90s before searching again.
    • When prey in its territory runs out, the pack moves its habitat to where prey is (migration), and may be pushed out by another carnivore pack with an overlapping territory.
