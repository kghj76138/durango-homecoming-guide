    #Overview UnverifiedLast changed 2026.09.29.3

    Plant seeds in a field (building), give water and fertilizer, and the crop grows over time. The moment it is fully grown a Survival check is made: if it survives it can be harvested, if it dies it must be pulled out. Numbers come from the original crops.txt·constants.farm·performance.txt; rules with no field in the fork's extracted data (crop level, harvest count, etc.) were filled from report measurements and fork estimates.

    • Each seed has a required Field size (small field 1 slot / large field 2 slots). Seeds that need a large field cannot be planted in a small one.
    • Each seed requires Farming technique node to plant (nodes costing 0 SP are automatic on reaching the category level). Harvesting needs no technique.
    • Water affects only growth speed and survival rate; soil fertility affects harvest count; soil affinity affects survival rate and the number of bonus seeds.

    #Crop levelLast changed 2026.09.29.3

    Crop level is set At the moment of planting. The fork's extracted data has no field for it, so this rule follows report measurements. Estimated

    Crop level = min( floor((seed + field) / 2), field + 4, seed + 2, planter's Farming technique level ) Harvest (and bonus seed) level = min( crop level, harvester's Farming technique level )
    • At most +2 over the seed per farming run — seed level rises only after growing several times in a good field (“60 field + 50 seed → formula gives 55, but measured 52”).
    • Water and fertilizer levels are ignored. Field level is the level of that field building.

    #Growth timeLast changed 2026.09.29.3

    Growth time is the original growth-time formula evaluated at Seed level, in seconds. Original (confirmed)

    Growth time = base growth formula(seed level) × (1 − min(0.3, Farming technique level × 0.005)) × (1 − encyclopedia growth reduction) While water is below the requirement, the crop grows at 0.5× speed.
    • Each Farming technique level shortens it by 0.5%, up to 30%. Estimated
    • Choosing the encyclopedia proficiency “Growth time reduction” (encyclopedia below) shortens it further (up to 90%).
    • Growth runs at half speed while water is short Estimated. Water fills up to the requirement when given and does not decrease afterwards.
    • “Instant Crop Growth” is free (offline). The survival check still happens.

    #Water and sprinklersLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    One water item (bottled water, bucket water, etc. with the water tag) gives 1 water. Only the crop's requirement is used; leftover water stays in the bag. Estimated

    • Meeting the requirement gives normal growth speed and maximum water satisfaction for the survival rate.
    • A Sprinkler waters fields in the surrounding watering tiles with each charge. Charges refill by 1 per cooldown, even while the game is off.
    • A Sprinkler with a Liquid Fertilizer tank also supplies the tank's liquid fertilizer, as much as is lacking, along with water (only on tiles marked for fertilizer).

    #Soil fertility and fertilizerLast changed 2026.09.29.3

    Putting a fertilizer item in a field accumulates that item's soil fertility. Fertilizer type and level only determine how much accumulates; only Accumulated soil fertility affects the harvest. Original (confirmed)

    Fertility one crop can hold = crop requirement + the field tile's surplus capacity Fertility left in the tile after harvest = amount added − requirement (the next crop inherits up to the capacity)

    Example: planting a crop requiring 20 in a level-60 small field (capacity 108) allows up to 128; if you put in 130, 110 remains after harvest and the next crop inherits 108 (capacity-108 cap example: 150 → 108). Estimated

    #Survival rateLast changed 2026.09.29.3

    When the crop finishes growing, one roll is made against a survival rate based on water satisfaction and soil affinity. Soil fertility does not affect the survival rate. If it survives it can be harvested; if it dies it withers and must be pulled up.

    Survival rate = clamp01( crop survivability × total score / 50 ) Original (confirmed) Total score = (25 + 2.5 × water satisfaction) × compatibility multiplier Estimated Satisfaction = min(1, amount added / required amount) × 10

    #HarvestLast changed 2026.09.29.5

    Base harvest count comes from the extra-yield field in the extracted data (1 + extra yield). Full fertilizer gives ×1.5 the base. Estimated

    Harvest count = max(1, round( (1 + extra yield) × (1 + 0.5 × fertility satisfaction ratio) )) If “Yield increase” was chosen in the encyclopedia: × (1 + that ratio) (rounded, never lower) Seeds produced = 2 if fertility satisfaction ratio + compatibility/3 is 1 or more, otherwise 1 Estimated

    #Farming encyclopedia and proficiencyLast changed 2026.09.29.3

    Each crop has a Farming encyclopedia, and its encyclopedia EXP rises by 1 per harvest. At encyclopedia levels 5·10·15·20 you Choose one of two proficiencies, applied to that crop only. Original (confirmed)

    Proficiency options — only the values may differ per crop (see the list table)

    Encyclopedia levelChosen proficiency(Corn example)
    Lv5Water requirement 1 or fertilizer requirement 4
    Lv10Gathering time 0.2 or gathering energy 0.2
    Lv15Growth time 0.1 or yield 0.1
    Lv20Latent attribute chance 0.2 or Rare attribute chance 0.2
    • Water/fertilizer requirement reductions are absolute values (times·fertility); growth/harvest time reductions and harvest increase are ratios.
    • Harvests needed per level go linearly with level between the two numbers in the encyclopedia table id (e.g. 130–149 → 130–149) as min and max, divided by the max level. Estimated
