    #HiringLast changed 2026.10.10.7

    Mercenaries are not in the original game. The former NPC clan members were turned into a hiring system by developer decision, and every value is newly set Changed from original (developer decision). At the clan board's “Hire mercenary” you pick one of 3 candidates (you need a clan).

    Max members
    10 (following + resting mercenaries combined)
    Price
    Each further slot costs more. Slots 1–8 cost T-Stones, slot 9 onward Warp Gems
    Price multiplier by grade
    S ×3 · A ×1.8 · B ×1 · C ×0.5 (of the B price; same multipliers for Warp Gem slots)
    On someone else's island
    Visiting a friend's island (hosted on another PC) as a guest, only your first 3 mercenaries appear. The rest stay on your roster and simply don't appear on that island. On the guest's own Tamed Island, all of them appear
    Level
    Mercenary level = owner level × 1

    #RerollsLast changed 2026.10.10.7

    If you don't like the candidates, “Reroll” gives 3 new ones Changed from original (developer decision).

    • Tries 1–4: T-Stones — 10%·20%·30%·50% of the slot's Grade B base price (min 300, rounded to 100s). Slots 9 and 10 use slot 8's base price.
    • From try 5: Warp Gems — starts at 20, rises each time, fixed at 100 from try 13.
    • The count resets to 0 only when you hire. Closing the window or changing islands keeps the same candidates and count.
    • There is no auto-reroll — you press it yourself each time.

    #EnhancementLast changed 2026.10.10.7

    At the clan board's “Enhance mercenary” you raise one mercenary one level at a time. Any grade can be enhanced; each level raises life and attack by a grade-dependent rate Changed from original (developer decision).

    • Max +15. From +16, zombie materials will be required later — not open yet.
    • Up to +10, a failure keeps the level. At +11 or higher, a failure has a 30% chance to drop one level (never below +10).
    • Pity: once consecutive failures reach that level's count, the next enhancement always succeeds. Success resets it to 0; a failure or drop adds +1.
    • Enhancement multiplier = 1 + level × gain per level. A knocked-down mercenary can't be enhanced.

    #Gear & bagLast changed 2026.10.10.7

    A hired mercenary arrives Bare-handed. The owner hands over weapons and armor in the clan board's “Mercenary weapons” window Changed from original (developer decision).

    Weapon
    Give a weapon from the owner's bag. Weapon performance and the attack-rating curve use the original data
    Armor
    Equip original armor in the head, body, gloves and shoes slots. The total defense reduces damage taken through the same attack-rating − defense curve as players (armor at 0 durability doesn't count). Same rule in single and multiplayer
    Bag
    Same window as the pet bag. Space by grade (sum of item sizes): C 10 · B 20 · A 30 · S 40
    Rest
    Rests while keeping equipped gear and bag contents
    Unstable islands
    When you leave an unstable island, unstable items in a mercenary's weapon, armor and bag vanish just like in your own bag

    #Knockdown & revivalLast changed 2026.10.10.7

    How a knocked-down mercenary gets back up depends on the island Changed from original (developer decision). Either way there is no permanent death.

    Normal islands (Tamed Island, etc.) — free

    • Gets up by itself after 60s with 50% life.
    • Owner rescue: 15s.
    • After 15s down, another mercenary goes to rescue it (CPR 1.5s).

    Unstable islands — T-Stone revival

    On unstable islands a mercenary doesn't get up by itself, through another mercenary's rescue, or through the owner's free rescue. The owner touches it and pays T-Stones by grade to revive it on the spot. If not revived, it gets back up when you leave the island.

    GradeRevive cost (T-Stones)
    Grade S500
    Grade A300
    Grade B200
    Grade C100

    #Rest & dismissalLast changed 2026.10.10.7

    Mercenaries are managed at the clan board Changed from original (developer decision).

    Rest
    Sends a mercenary to rest. It still counts toward your hired total. “Call” brings all resting mercenaries back for free
    Dismiss
    Dismiss one at a time. No refund. Given weapons, equipped armor and the mercenary's bag contents return to the owner's bag (nothing is dropped even if the bag is full)
    Former clan members
    The auto-recruited clan members from before the mercenary system were all disbanded once, and weapons given to them returned to the bag
