    Character stats

    Formulas turning the 8 base stats into combat, crafting, gathering and survival stats, per-level values, survival gauges, climate resistance, titles, and Level/EXP. Table values were produced by running the rule functions of the released game code (DLL) directly.

    Source grade — the badge next to a value shows its basis. Last changed next to a section title is the public version in which that section's content last changed, and links to Patch history.
    • Original (confirmed) A value taken directly from a row of the extracted original data with no signs of editing, or from the original client code — 17 on this page
    • Estimated A value the restoration filled in because the original data has no field for it (e.g. rules that were server-only) — 29 on this page
    • Changed from original (developer decision) A value that exists in the original but was set differently by developer decision — 12 on this page
    • Unverified Values not yet backed by a source — Shown in section title

    #Base statsLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    Characters have 8 base stats, from which combat, crafting, gathering and survival stats (derived stats) are calculated. In this restoration, a base stat is the sum of the following.

    • Level — all 8 start at the same value as the character level Estimated. The original server's per-level gains are not in the extracted data, so all are set to the same size, based on titles treating the 8 stats with the same budget (titles.txt).
    • 1 selected titlesstat bonus of Changed from original (developer decision) (Below Title)
    • Equipped gearof <Stats>_plus (Original performance.modifiers)
    • Food buff·Status effectof <Stats>_plus (Cooking Note)

    #Crafting·Gathering abilityLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    Crafting abilities (weapon, clothing, sewing, metalworking, cooking, furniture, construction, handicraft) are 35 + ⌊(Average of related base stats + 2 × Level) ÷ 6⌋ Estimated. The formula fits tester measurements (Lv 60, no titles: weapon 72, furniture 65, construction 65, cooking 82); since original base stats are about 180 at Lv 60 (≈3×level), the fork's base stats (equal to level) are topped up by the missing 2×level to measure on the original scale. Farming and the four gathering abilities (plant gathering, mining, butchering, disassembly) are the average of related base stats as-is.

    On top of this come the *_plus from titles, gear, learned technique nodes (up to +20 per field), food and status effects (up to +12 from Tamed Island research). “All crafting abilities” applies equally to the 7 of handicraft, weapon, clothing, sewing, metalworking, cooking and farming; “All construction abilities” to furniture, construction and farming; “All gathering abilities” to the 4 of disassembly, plant gathering, butchering and mining.

    Fatigue grade

    When Fatigue passes certain fractions of its max, a tiered status effect is applied (updated every 59s). Tired and Exhausted rise 1 tier for every 180s spent in that range and drain Life and Energy. Exhausted only clears once you recover back to the Tired range. The two Strained and Weary ranges, plus the caps and intervals, were set by the fork Estimated. Before you claim a Domain (the intro), Fatigue does not build up at all Estimated.

    Fatigue recovery from rest (rest*) status effects is ×3 the original value Changed from original (developer decision), and normal rest recovers Life and Health by at least Per second +1 Changed from original (developer decision). Moving ends the rest.

    #Death and revivalLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    On death, Health, Life, Energy and Stamina revive at a fixed fraction of their max depending on recent death count, and Fatigue drops by a fixed fraction of its max Original (confirmed).

    Bag item drop

    Each item in your bag drops with probability 0.5 × 0.0125 × Level (0–0.5) Original (confirmed) (Lv 1: 0.625%, Lv 40: 25%, Lv 60: 37.5%). Untradable items, items used as weapons, clothing, armor and bags never drop, and locking does not protect items Estimated. On death, every item in your bag loses 3 Durability Estimated. The drop spot remains for 300s.

    Instant Revival

    Instant Revival is Free Estimated (the original cost 100 Warp Gems or a revival ticket). Gauges return at 70% of max, and the Instant Revival Sickness status effect lasts 600s (original: 30s) Estimated.

    #Climate and resistanceLast changed 2026.09.24.5

Snow adds Chill and heavy rain adds Humid, and at an unstable sailing factor of 2 or higher Heat and Chill escalate to Intense Heat and Intense Chill Estimated.

    Formula

    remaining ratio = base multiplier × max(0, 1 − resistance ÷ island level) effect level = island level × 0.35 × remaining ratio fatigue per second = remaining ratio × 0.5 × max(1, effect level) ÷ 60 × (1 + status effect multiplier)

    Effect levels and fatigue formulas are the original constants as-is Original (confirmed); the way resistance cuts the ratio (resistance ÷ island level) is an interpretation Estimated. With resistance equal to the island level, that category's fatigue is 0.
