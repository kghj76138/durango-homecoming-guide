    #Categories and learning rulesLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    Techniques are divided into 13 Category(Category). Each category has a level (1–60); doing that category's actions (hunting, gathering, crafting…) raises category EXP and levels it up. Technique nodes are learned by meeting the Category level requirement and spending Skill Point(SP) Original (confirmed).

    • Within one line (technique), each tier must be learned before the next.
    • “Branches” (side branches such as Critical hit or duration) open only once you have that technique's Base row stage 1.
    • Nodes costing 0 SP (“AUTO”) are learned automatically the moment the category level is reached Estimated — see “Auto Acquisition” below.
    • Resetting techniques is Unlimited·Free and refunds spent SP Changed from original (developer decision) (the original: 15 times a day, 30 Warp Gems after 5 times).
    • SP is earned by leveling up — per-level table in Character stats “Level and EXP”. Learning all 918 original nodes takes 3,213 SP, and this restoration is tuned so cumulative SP at Lv 60 is slightly more than that.

    #Category level·EXP·ResearchLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    Category EXP needed to level a category uses a shared table; Processing needs about half of it, and Surviving Toxic Oil Islands has its own Original (confirmed). A single action raises category EXP by at most 3.

    At levels 20·25·30·35·40·45·50·55·59, full EXP does not level you up immediately; you must finish Research to reach the next level (Survival and Surviving Toxic Oil Islands have no research). Research time is multiplied by this restoration's work time multiplier ×0.5 Changed from original (developer decision). Only one category can be researched at a time Estimated, and while researching, the remaining time is cut by EXP gained in that category × (level ÷ 10) seconds (up to 25% of the total) Original (confirmed).

    #Effects of technique levelLast changed 2026.09.27.10

    Category (technique) level affects not only which nodes you can learn but also Work speed and Crafting result level. All work-time scaling values are set by this restoration Changed from original (developer decision). All times already include the work time multiplier ×0.5.

    Gathering·Butchering·Harvest time

    Time = base time × (1 − gear/status effect reduction) × (1 − 20% × technique ramp) × (1 − 25% × tool ramp), never below 30% of base. Ramp = (level − 1) ÷ 59, so 0 at Lv 1 and 1 at Lv 60. Technique is the job's category level; tool is the level at which the tool carries the required tag (bare-handed 0). Base time depends on target type and item level (+1% per level); details are on the Gathering page.

    Gathering technique lock

    Some Gatherables can only be gathered after learning the matching “Gathering allowed” node (Effect column in the table above).
