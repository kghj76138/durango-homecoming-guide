    #Getting the Ragdoll companion UnverifiedLast changed 2026.09.28.1

    Added in public client 2026.09.28.1. You receive Ragdoll Reins once as the reward for reaching character Lv 1 in the “New Companion” quest. Bind the reins in your bag, then summon it from the animal list. Re-requesting the reward or reconnecting does not grant the reins again.

    A companion using appearance and animations added by the developer. It reuses the existing non-riding, non-combat pet rules and is not presented as a restored cat from the original. It is not obtained by catching a wild Ragdoll. Therizinosaurus and regular Ragdolls are excluded from wild spawning and from restoring saved wild animals.

    Quest conditions and rewards

    #OverviewLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    Instead of knocking a wild animal down, capture it with Rein and tame it in a Pen (growth pen) to make it a pet. Pets follow you and fight alongside you (95 species), can be ridden (93 species), or produce items such as eggs, milk and feathers through training and production tasks. The original pet data contains 111 species.

    • Max level 60 Original (confirmed), Max lifespan 60 d Changed from original (developer decision), Number that can be released on one island 5 Original (confirmed).
    • Grade (S·A·B) is rolled on successful taming using Nexon's official published probabilities Changed from original (developer decision).
    • Pet Attack/Defense/Accuracy window values, total taming time, base production quantity and feed compatibility multipliers are Estimated, as the original server values are unavailable.

    #TamingLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    Steps

    • Capture a wild animal by using Rein on it (only tameable species have reins — see “Taming” in the Animal encyclopedia).
    • Put the reins into the Pen(Growth pen†) and start taming. When the total time has passed, a success roll is made; on success the animal becomes a pet.
    • Adding feed while waiting shortens the remaining time and raises the success chance, and the feed's stat tags stack with a chance.

    Total time

    Total time = 1800s × (1 + reins size ÷ 10) × work time multiplier 0.5

    Size is the reins data's size (10–120) Estimated. The work time multiplier 0.5 is the restoration's “work 2× faster” Changed from original (developer decision).

    #Feed·Vigor·Stat tagsLast changed 2026.09.27.11

    A pet's Vigor is the gauge used for tasks and combat. For most species it drains by -0.09 per second (original reins hungry_velocity), and feeding refills it by the vigor of the feed's level bracket Original (confirmed).

    Feed has task time reduction (decrease_grow_time), taming reduction/chance and Stat tag values. Feed given while taming in a Pen rolls each tag by chance as before. Feed given directly to a tamed pet does not attach tags immediately but banks them toward the next Signpost Changed from original (developer decision).

    Banked value per tag = min(0.50, previous banked value + this feed's pet_food value) Next Signpost success candidate weight = 1 + 25 × banked value Chance within candidates = that weight ÷ sum of all tag weights × 65%

    The 35% Signpost failure chance is unchanged. Whether it succeeds or fails, banked values are consumed once used Changed from original (developer decision). Tag effects are restoration values Estimated: …_plus +10 each, …_amplifier ×1.1 each, Vigor-drain reduction tags — per second.

    The two “Infinite Vigor” items (unlimited_pet_vigor, unlimited_pet_vigor_1d) replace each other, and Vigor does not drain while active.

    #CombatLast changed 2026.09.24.5

    Only species that can fight (original is_fightable, 95 species) fight. Pets counterattack wild animals that bite their owner Original (confirmed).

    • To start fighting, Vigor must be above 50% of the maximum; at 0 Vigor the pet drops out Original (confirmed).
    • Each attack uses 2 Vigor. The attack interval is the attack_cooltime of the wild species the pet came from (2s if none) Estimated.
    • Hit chance = (0.9 − target Evasion ÷ 1000) × (1 + Accuracy amplifier), clamped to 0.3–0.95 Estimated.
    • Damage = pet species' Attack × 1/(1 + target Defense/400) (0.15–1), minimum 1 Estimated.
    • Pet Defense when hit by wild animals = level × 5 (half when old). The Defense value shown in the window is not used in combat.
    • Injured pets recover to full over 300s, and downed pets can only be revived with Animal Medicine (medicine_animal) Estimated.
    • Wild animals target pets more than the owner by the original pet_aggro_ratio (mostly 1, 3 for the four giant ankylos).
